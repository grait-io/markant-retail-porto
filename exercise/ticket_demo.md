# Ticket PRC-214: Tier pricing in the article views

*Fictional ticket for the workshop. Not Markant code or data.*

As a category manager I want to see tier prices for an article, so that I can
check what a supplier's volume discount means per unit.

- Show the tier price in the article **edit view** and in the **display view**.
- Tiers: from 10 units 5 % off, from 100 units 12 % off.
- The total for the entered quantity should be shown as well.
- Prices should look right for our markets.

Project: `pricing-demo/`. Run `npm test` before and after.
